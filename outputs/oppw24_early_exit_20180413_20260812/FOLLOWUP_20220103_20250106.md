## Conclusion

Idea 41 is too aggressive for these windows: its drawdown reduction comes with an extreme CAGR penalty. The new OR rule is too rare to protect plain leverage override and is counterproductive for protected mode. Neither is recommended under the requirement that CAGR reduction is acceptable only when drawdown materially improves.
