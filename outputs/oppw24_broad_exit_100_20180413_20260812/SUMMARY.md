# Protected OPPW24: 100 broad exit ideas

## Scope

- Protected regime with leverage override, gap-momentum, Tuesday normalization, and premarket-low.
- Inclusive range: 2018-04-13 through 2026-08-12.
- Initial balance 10,000; no top-ups; tax enabled.
- Four families, 25 ideas each: intraday time stops, multi-day deadlines, profit giveback, and moving-average failure.
- All decisions use information available at the exit minute and execute at the current M1 close.

| CAGR | Daily marked-equity DD | Closed-trade DD |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 851.217% | 84.672% | 82.076% |

Fifty-eight of 100 ideas improved daily drawdown.

## Conclusion

No broad idea is robust enough to replace structural idea 80 from the preceding sweep. Idea 18 consistently reduces drawdown but sacrifices too much CAGR. Idea 60 retains more full-history CAGR and is excellent in the 2025-start window, but its 2022-start drawdown improvement is only 0.330 percentage points while CAGR falls by 256.376 points. That instability makes it unsuitable as the primary rule without a genuinely held-out validation period.
